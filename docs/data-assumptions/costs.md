# Technology Costs & Settings

**Where each cost in a model comes from**

| What                                          | Source                                                                                                                                                                                       | Described in                                                         |
|----------------------|-----------------------------|----------------------|
| New thermal plants, heat plants and batteries | One cost table assembled from IEA World Energy Outlook 2025, NREL Annual Technology Baseline 2024, Danish Energy Agency catalogues and others, in US dollars of 2022, for nine world regions | [New Technology Characterization](../methods/new-technologies.md)                |
| Cost of capital                               | Damodaran industry and country data                                                                                                                                                          | [New Technology Characterization](../methods/new-technologies.md)                |
| Existing plants                               | Costs and efficiencies by technology, size, region and vintage                                                                                                                               | [Existing Stock Characterization](../methods/existing-stock-characterization.md) |
| Wind and solar                                | Capital cost from the same cost table; connection cost from the distance between each resource cluster and its grid node                                                                     | [Renewable Energy Characterization](../methods/renewable-characterization.md)      |
| Grid corridors                                | 1.1 M\$ per GW per km; losses 0.006% per km                                                                                                                                                  | [Grid Representation](../methods/grid-representation.md)             |
| Fuel and carbon prices                        | Country base-year ranges; IPCC AR6 regional pathways                                                                                                                                         | [Demands and Prices](../methods/demands-and-prices.md)              |

All sources and their versions are listed in [Source Datasets](sources.md).
